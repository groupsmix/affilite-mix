# Legal & Privacy Checklist

## Required Legal Documents
- [ ] **Privacy Policy:** Current and clearly linked in the footer.
- [ ] **Terms of Service:** Up-to-date for merchants and users.
- [ ] **Affiliate Disclosure:** Automatically generated on all product pages (`app/(public)/components/ProductCard.tsx`).
- [ ] **Data Processing Agreement (DPA):** Executed with Cloudflare, Supabase, Stripe, and Resend.
- [ ] **Retention Policy:** Detailed in `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md` (90 days HTTP, 365 days Audit).
- [ ] **Deletion SLA:** Documented right-to-delete jobs and API paths.
- [ ] **Cookie Policy:** Cookie banner implemented for tracking/analytics opt-in.
- [ ] **Security Contact:** `security@groupsmix.com` added to `security.txt` in the root of the `.well-known` directory.

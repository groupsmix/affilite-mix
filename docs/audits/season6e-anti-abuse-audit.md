# Season 6e — Anti-Abuse, Fraud & Trust Safety Audit

**Repository:** `groupsmix/affilite-mix`  
**Branch:** `main`  
**Auditor:** Devin (principal engineer)  
**Date:** 2026-05-29  
**Scope:** 11 audit checks (A152 – A162)

---

## Executive Summary

The platform has strong foundational security controls: rate limiting with fail-closed policy on critical paths, HIBP breached-password checks, TOTP 2FA, JWT binding, disposable-email blocking, distributed rate limiting via Durable Objects, and comprehensive audit logging with PII redaction. Several gaps remain — primarily around the absence of device fingerprinting, lack of 3DS2/SCA enforcement at the Stripe integration level, missing referral/loyalty abuse controls (no such system exists yet), and the absence of sanctions/KYC screening (not currently required for the content/membership model).
